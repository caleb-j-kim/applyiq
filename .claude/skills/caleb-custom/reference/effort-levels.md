# Interview prep effort levels

`interview-prep-template-example.txt` is the **high-effort** reference. Lower levels keep its
substance but drop sections that repeat each other, so a request only spends the tokens the user
chose. Every level starts with **Interviewer Information** (name and email first, then current
title, previous titles, education, location and a talking point from public profile information
the user supplied or that can be verified).

The frontend mock (`frontend/src/lib/demo-ai.ts`) follows these section lists exactly.

## Low effort (quick refresher, roughly 1 page)

1. Interviewer Information
2. Key Points: what the role is, top focus areas, best positioning, strongest evidence
3. Biggest Gaps — and How to Answer Them
4. Likely Questions: the highest-value recruiter and technical questions, with short answers
5. Questions to Ask: recruiter and hiring manager combined, 3-5 total

## Medium effort (full prep, no redundancy)

Merges the high-effort sections that overlap:

1. Interviewer Information
2. Qualifications: fit label + evidence per requirement; gap answers live here (replaces a separate Biggest Gaps section)
3. Company Research: what they do, how the role contributes, a spoken "why this company/role"
4. Key Points: positioning and the top three pieces of evidence (no repeated skill lists)
5. Knowledge: the 4-6 topics most likely to be tested, each with a likely question
6. Behavioral Stories to Prepare
7. Likely Questions: recruiter, hiring manager and scenario questions with answers (replaces three separate sections)
8. Interview Strategy: clarifying-question steps + coding strategy (replaces Clarifying Questions, Data Structures Recap and Coding Interview Strategy)
9. Questions to Ask: recruiter and hiring manager, grouped
10. Interview Notes Placeholders

## High effort (the full template)

Interviewer Information, then all 14 sections from SKILL.md in order, plus Likely Recruiter
Questions (with answers) as in the reference: Qualifications, Company Research, Clarifying
Questions / Interview Approach, Data Structures Recap, Key Points, Biggest Gaps — and How to
Answer Them, Knowledge, Behavioral Stories to Prepare, Likely Recruiter Questions, Likely
Technical / Hiring Manager Questions, Technical Scenario Questions, Coding Interview Strategy,
Questions for the Recruiter, Questions for the Hiring Manager, Interview Notes Placeholders.
